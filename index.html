A Trusted Engineering Consultant

Pressure Vessel Engineering specializes in design and detailed engineering services for pressure vessels, heat exchangers, storage tanks and other industrial static equipment. We support equipment manufacturers, fabrication companies, EPC contractors, engineering consultants and plant owners with mechanical design calculations, technical drawings and engineering documentation tailored to project requirements. Our approach combines engineering principles, applicable design codes and fabrication-oriented detailing to help clients achieve accurate designs, efficient manufacturing and well-coordinated project execution. From individual equipment assignments to complete engineering packages, PV Eng is committed to delivering practical, reliable and professionally documented engineering solutions.

Our Engineering Capabilities

Pressure Vessel Design

Design and code calculations for separators, reactors, columns, air receivers, Agitators, Filter Vessels and process vessels in accordance with applicable design codes and project specifications.

ASME Section VIII Div. 1 & Div. 2

Heat Exchanger Design

Thermal and mechanical design support, component calculations, tubesheet design and detailed engineering for shell-and-tube heat exchangers / Electrical Process Heaters.

TEMA & ASME

Storage Tank Design

Engineering calculations and detailing for atmospheric and low-pressure storage tanks, including shell, roof, bottom, nozzles and structural components.

API 650 & API 620

Detailed Engineering

General arrangement drawings, fabrication drawings, nozzle orientation details, weld maps, bills of materials and material take-off schedules.

GA • Fabrication • BOM • MTO

Stress Analysis & FEA

Engineering assessment of applicable local loads, nozzle loads, lifting conditions, transportation loads and structural integrity.

Analysis as per project scope

Design Review & Modification

Design verification, drawing reviews, equipment modification support and engineering assessments for existing equipment, subject to applicable codes and scope.
