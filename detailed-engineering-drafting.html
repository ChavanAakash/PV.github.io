Detailed Engineering & Drafting Scope

Detailed Engineering & Drafting Services

Pressure Vessel Engineering converts approved design inputs into coordinated fabrication documentation that supports manufacturing, assembly, inspection and project execution. Our detailed engineering and drafting services focus on clear general arrangement and assembly drawings, component and nozzle details, weld maps, material take-offs, bills of materials and project-specific notes. We coordinate drawing information with applicable specifications and design requirements, helping fabricators and project teams work from consistent, practical documentation through revisions and, where included, as-built updates.

Answers to Common
Detailed Engineering & Drafting Queries.

Here are answers to common questions about fabrication drawings, engineering documentation and drafting deliverables.

We typically work from approved design calculations, datasheets, project specifications, material requirements, equipment outlines and applicable codes. Existing mark-ups or reference drawings can also be used where available.
Depending on project requirements, deliverables may include general arrangement and assembly drawings, shop and component drawings, nozzle orientation schedules, weld maps, bills of materials, material take-offs and cutting lists.
Weld maps, specified NDE requirements and general notes can be incorporated based on the approved welding documentation, project specification and applicable codes. The exact requirements are confirmed with the client.
Yes. Revision updates and as-built drawings can be included when they form part of the agreed project scope. Updated information is coordinated against approved comments and manufacturing or site feedback supplied to us.
